## Review with evidence

| Score | Next action |
|---|---|
| Below 70% | Repeat the mapped tasks and explain the observed behavior |
| 70–84% | Review missed objectives and repeat their negative checks |
| 85% or higher | Move forward when practical validation, break/fix, and cleanup are complete |

These are study thresholds. They do not predict an official exam result.
